Design references: Ableton Arpeggiator/MIDI Tools (pattern order, synced rate, gate, transposition); Bitwig Arpeggiator + Operators/Note Repeats (octave behavior, cycle/probability logic, Euclidean patterns); Godfried Toussaint’s work on maximally-even Euclidean rhythms.

What the MPE engine does: every sounding note receives its own member channel. Pitch uses per-note Pitch Bend, brightness uses CC74, and pressure uses Channel Pressure. Common tones stay calm; leading tones and semitone resolutions may glide toward their destination; unresolved extensions get more timbre movement; top voices are more expressive than bass notes. The exported MIDI includes MPE zone configuration and member-channel pitch-bend sensitivity.
